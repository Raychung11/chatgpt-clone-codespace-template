verified resale burial plots, columbarium niches, and dignified funeral planning.

## The Problem We Solve

Buying or selling a burial plot in Malaysia has always been:

- **Opaque** — prices hidden, no standardised listings
- **Unverified** — no way to check ownership authenticity before paying
- **Stressful** — families in grief rushed into decisions without information
- **Fragmented** — no single platform for plots, niches, providers, and planning

**PlotGold fixes all of this.**

## Frequently Asked Questions (for Content)

**Is PlotGold free to use?**
Browsing and all buyer features are completely free. Sellers list for free.
Premium seller and provider features are available on subscription.

**How does plot verification work?**
Sellers upload their deed and ownership documents. Our team reviews and
assigns a verification score. Fully verified listings display a gold badge
and attract significantly more serious enquiries.

**What states do you cover?**
Currently focused on Klang Valley (KL, Selangor, Putrajaya). Expansion
to Johor, Penang, Ipoh and other states is in progress.

**Can I list an inherited plot?**
Yes. We accept listings from estate executors and beneficiaries.
A Grant of Probate or Letter of Administration may be required.

**How long does the transfer process take?**
Most memorial parks complete the ownership transfer within 4–8 weeks
once all documents are submitted.

**Do you offer urgent funeral support?**
Yes — our bereavement support team is available 24/7 via WhatsApp
for urgent cases. No forms required for immediate assistance.
