## 1. License Position

**No additional license is required** for gold vault storage when structured as
**free optional storage** for gold that customers already own (retail sale model).

Kasih AP Gold, Public Gold, and Quantum Metal all operate this way using
**SSM + Premise License only**.

### Why Vault Storage is License-Free

```
Your Model: "Buy RM1,500 gold → We store FREE in vault"
Legal View: Retail sale + free customer service
(No "financial custody" → No SC/BNM license required)
```

### The Four-Point Legal Structure

| # | Condition | Implication |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Customer owns physical gold** (stated clearly in T&Cs) | No custody relationship under Capital Markets Act |
| 2 | **Storage is free** (like free parking at a shop) | Not a financial service — no SC/BNM trigger |
| 3 | **No guaranteed returns** (gold price risk = customer's) | Not a deposit product — no BNM trigger |
| 4 | **Audited vaults** (insured, fireproof — show certificates) | Demonstrates duty of care without financial licensing |

### What Triggers SC/BNM (Avoid These)

- ❌ Promising fixed or guaranteed returns
- ❌ Taking fiat custody (holding customer's cash without disbursing gold)
- ❌ Offering a "gold investment fund" or pooled vehicle
- ✅ **Sell physical gold + provide free storage = 100% SSM safe**

## 5. Copywriting Guidelines

All customer-facing copy for the gold vault feature must follow these rules.

### Tone of Voice
- **Reassuring, not speculative** — customers are storing wealth, not gambling
- **Transparent** — be explicit about ownership, no hidden fees, no guaranteed returns
- **Professional but warm** — families trust us; speak like a trusted advisor
- **Compliance-first** — never imply investment returns or capital appreciation

### Approved Language ✅

| Instead of… | Use… |
|------------|------|
| "Invest in gold" | "Save in gold" / "Own physical gold" |
| "Earn returns" | "Track your gold's market value" |
| "Gold investment plan" | "Gold savings plan" / "Physical gold ownership" |
| "We hold your money" | "You own the gold; we store it free" |
| "Guaranteed value" | "Real gold, real ownership" |
| "Grow your wealth" | "Protect your wealth with gold" |

### Headline Formulas ✅
- `"Own [X]g of physical gold. We store it free."`
- `"Buy RM[X] of gold. Store it in our vault at no cost."`
- `"Your gold. Your ownership. Free vault. No lock-in."`
- `"Start with RM500. Own real PAMP-certified gold today."`

### Phrases to Avoid ❌
- "Invest" / "Investment" (implies regulated financial product)
- "Returns" / "Yield" / "Profit" (triggers SC scrutiny)
- "We safeguard your money" (implies fiat custody)
- "Earn" in relation to gold value
- "Capital growth" / "Appreciation guaranteed"

## 7. PDPA Compliance Notes

When collecting customer data for gold accounts:

- Collect only: name, IC/passport, contact, delivery address (for redemption)
- State purpose clearly: "For gold ownership records and redemption processing"
- Do not share data with third parties without consent
- Provide data access/correction request mechanism
- Retain records per SSM requirement (7 years minimum)

## 8. Shariah Compliance Structure

For Islamic customers, the model must satisfy **Qabdh** (possession) requirements:

1. **Immediate constructive possession** — gold is allocated to the customer's account at point of purchase
2. **Specific gold** — each customer's holding is traceable to a serial/lot number
3. **No commingling** — customer gold is segregated, not pooled
4. **No riba element** — no interest, no deferred payment, no guaranteed returns

> Recommended: Engage a Shariah advisor to issue a letter confirming compliance
> before launching to Islamic customers.
